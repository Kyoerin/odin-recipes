Banana Bread


Home

Description

A soft yet moist banana bread, that'll have your kids or spouse asking for the bananas to go over-ripe just so you'll make it!

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 Teaspoons baking soda
  • 1/2 Teaspoons Kosher salt
  • 2 cups mashed ripe bananas. (4 medium)
  • 1 Teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 cup butter (1 stick), softened
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 325 degrees F. Grease 8 1/2"-x 1 1/2" metal loaf pan. In a medium bowl, with wire whisk, stir together flour, baking soda, and salt. In a small bowl, stir together bananas and vanilla until blended.
  2. In a large bowl, with mixer on medium speed, beat butter and both sugars until light and fluffy. Beat in eggs, 1 at a time. Reduce speed to low; alternately add flour mixture and banana mixture, beginning and ending with flour mixture and occasionally scraping bowl with rubber spatula. Beat batter just until blended.
  3. Pour batter into prepared pan. Bake until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean, about 1 hour. Cool loaf in pan on wire rack 10 minutes; remove from pan and cool completely on rack before slicing.
