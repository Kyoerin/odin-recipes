Grilled Chicken Thighs


Home

Description

Grilled Chicken Thighs are made ultra juicy with my powerful tenderizing marinade ingredients. Flavourful with irresistible crispy bits.

Ingredients

For Honey Mustard sauce

  • 1/2 cup dijon mustard
  • 1/4 cup honey

For Mustard marinade

  • 1/4 cup full-fat Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup lemon juice
  • 2 Tablespoons minced garlic
  • 2 Tablespoons cooking oil (avocado or canola oil) - don't use a low smoke-point oil like olive oil
  • 2 1/2 Teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 Teaspoon paprika
  • 1/4 Teaspoons ground cayenne
  • 3 lbs bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs

Steps

Make a Honey Mustard sauce

  1. In a small bowl, whisk together 1/4 cup of the mustard and the honey. Set aside. (Refrigerate it if you are doing a marinade longer than a couple of hours.)

  1. Whisk remaining mustard with yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, oil, salt,paprika, and cayenne in a large baking dish. Add the chicken thighs and turn to coat. Cover the dish with plastic wrap and marinade chicken for 30 minutes at room temperature (or up to 24 hours in the refrigerator- the flavour gets even better if you can do this!) Either way, let the chicken stand at room temperature for 30 minutes just before cooking for best results.

  1. Turn burners on a gas grill to medium heat and preheat the grill for 5-10 minutes with the lid closed. Then, carefully oil the grates using long tongs and a paper-towel dabbed in oil.

Grill the Chicken Thighs

  1. Remove chicken from the marindae letting any excess drip off (then discard it). Grill chicken skin-side down for about 7 minutes or until the pieces lift easily from the grates without sticking or tearing.
  2. Turn off HALF of the burners and flip the chicken pieces over and onto the until side. Then turn the side with *no chicken* on to high heat. (You're basically creating an oven to finish cooking the chicken without burning it).
  3. Cover the grill and continue cooking the chicken until it reaches 165 degrees F internal temperature.

Brush with the Honey Mustard Sauce

  1. Brush the chicken pieces on both sides with the reserved honey mustard sauce and continue cooking over indirect heat (unlit side) with cover closed for 5-10 minutes more, until the glaze is caramelized and internal temperature reaches 175 degress F.

Rest the Chicken

  1. Let the chicken rest on a plate, uncovered, for 5-10 minutes before serving for juiciest results. The cooking time with be 15-25 minutes in total.
